# Daily Servings By Calorie Level
*Dietary Guidelines for Americans, 2025–2030*

The following serving targets are based on calorie level. Foods should be nutrient-dense, with no or limited added sugars, refined carbohydrates, or chemical additives. The calories you need depend on your age, sex, height, weight, and level of physical activity.

**Serving size references:** Protein Foods = 3 oz cooked meat, poultry, or seafood; 1 egg; ½ cup beans, peas, or lentils; 1 oz nuts or seeds; 2 tbsp nut/seed butter. Dairy = 1 cup milk; ¾ cup yogurt; 1 oz cheese. Vegetables = 1 cup raw or cooked; 2 cups leafy greens. Fruits = 1 cup raw; ½ cup dried. Whole Grains = ½ cup cooked oats, brown rice, barley, quinoa, or buckwheat; 1 slice bread; 1 tortilla. Healthy Fats = 1 tsp olive oil or butter.

## FDA Added Sugar Limits by Food Group

To qualify as "healthy," foods must not exceed the following added sugar thresholds per serving:

Grain products (¾ oz whole-grain equivalent): maximum 5g added sugar. Dairy products (⅔ cup equivalent): maximum 2.5g added sugar. Vegetable products (½ cup equivalent): maximum 1g added sugar. Fruit products (½ cup equivalent): maximum 1g added sugar. Game meat (1½ oz equivalent): maximum 1g added sugar. Seafood (1 oz equivalent): maximum 1g added sugar. Eggs (1 egg): maximum 1g added sugar. Beans, peas, and lentils (1 oz equivalent): maximum 1g added sugar. Nuts, seeds, and soy products (1 oz equivalent): maximum 1g added sugar.
